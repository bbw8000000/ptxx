**DexTools visibility campaigns delivered through the official DexMoji Telegram bot**

DexMoji publishes DexTools Trending support across eligible chains, with advertised placement options in the Top 1–10 range. This repository provides product information rather than downloadable software. The production Telegram bot, campaign systems and placement infrastructure remain private.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> DexTools Trending is paid promotional visibility. A trending position does not prove organic demand, token quality, contract safety or community adoption. It cannot guarantee buyers, price growth, liquidity or financial return.

## What a DexTools Placement Changes

A trending placement changes where an eligible project appears within DexTools discovery areas during the campaign period. This can increase the number of people who encounter the token page.

The placement itself does not control what happens after a visitor arrives. Visitors may inspect:

- Price charts
- Trading pairs
- Liquidity information
- Transaction history
- Contract details
- Token links and branding
- Holder or market information provided by connected services

Projects should prepare these public surfaces before increasing exposure. Missing links, incorrect contracts or inconsistent branding can waste campaign attention.

## DexTools Campaign Readiness

Use a readiness review before opening an order.

### Confirm the exact pair

Verify the blockchain, contract address, exchange and trading pair. Similar token names can exist across different networks and pools.

### Review the token page

Check the displayed name, ticker, logo, website and public project information. A trending campaign can attract attention to both strong and weak presentation details.

### Choose a meaningful window

Connect the campaign to a legitimate reason for visibility, such as a documented project update, product release, community event or prepared marketing campaign.

### Set a measurable target

Decide what will be recorded: position reached, duration, page views, referrals, engagement or another observable metric.

### Define success correctly

Delivery success means that the booked visibility was provided according to the confirmed campaign. It does not mean that token price or trading activity must increase.

## Measuring a DexTools Trending Campaign

Measurement should separate placement delivery from wider marketing impact.

### Placement delivery

Record:

- Requested range
- Position reached
- Time first observed
- Campaign completion time
- Any delivery evidence supplied with the order

### Visibility response

Where data is available, compare:

- Page views before and during the campaign
- Referral traffic
- Website visits
- Community-channel activity
- Engagement with current project announcements

### Post-campaign behavior

After paid visibility ends, review whether visitors continued exploring the project. This helps distinguish temporary exposure from sustained interest.

Short-term token price should not be the only campaign metric. Price can move for many reasons outside a visibility provider's control.

## DexTools Trending Versus DexScreener Trending

DexTools and DexScreener are separate platforms with different interfaces, audiences and discovery systems.

| Question | DexTools Trending | DexScreener Trending |

A DexTools campaign does not automatically create DexScreener visibility. Projects interested in both should confirm and measure two separate orders or campaign components.

## Choosing DexTools Over Another Platform

DexTools may be a useful primary destination when:

- The project's audience already uses DexTools
- The relevant chain and pair are supported
- The DexTools token page is complete
- The team wants to compare discovery platforms
- The campaign objective is specifically DexTools visibility

Another destination may be more suitable when the intended audience primarily uses a different terminal, explorer or wallet.

The right choice depends on audience relevance, not only the advertised ranking number.

## Common Campaign Mistakes

### Sending the wrong contract

Projects with similar names or multiple deployments can be confused. Verify every character and network.

### Assuming every platform is bundled

DexTools, DexScreener and terminal placements are separate destinations. Confirm exactly what is included.

### Measuring only price

Price can be influenced by market conditions, liquidity, holders and unrelated trading. Measure the placement itself first.

### Starting before the page is ready

Trending sends attention to the existing token page. It does not correct incomplete branding, outdated links or unclear project information.

### Treating paid visibility as organic

Keep campaign-driven metrics separate from independent community and discovery data.

## Responsible Reporting

Campaign reports should use precise language.

Appropriate statements include:

- The project purchased a DexTools Trending campaign.
- The campaign reached the recorded placement.
- Visibility or referral activity changed during the campaign.

Avoid unsupported statements such as:

- Trending proves organic demand.
- DexTools endorsed the project.
- The placement guarantees token growth.
- Paid visibility confirms contract safety.
- Every reaction or visit represents a unique investor.

Paid promotion should be disclosed where required by law, platform policy or publication context.
