# DexMoji - DexScreener Reactions Bot

**Rocket, fire, poop, red-flag and watchlist reaction campaigns for DexScreener token pages**


  
  
  


  


The **DexMoji DexScreener Reactions Bot** provides promotional reaction campaigns for eligible token pages on DexScreener. Projects can select from the published reaction categories and arrange delivery through DexMoji's official Telegram bot.

This repository focuses on reactions rather than trending placement. It explains the available reaction types, how to verify the correct DexScreener page, how to record delivery and how to distinguish paid engagement from independent community sentiment.

DexMoji is a hosted service. This repository does not include the production bot, reaction-delivery infrastructure, traffic systems or private operational tools.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> DexScreener reactions purchased through a promotional service are paid engagement signals. They must not be represented as independent votes, authentic community opinion, real holders or guaranteed market demand.

## Published Reaction Types

DexMoji lists support for five DexScreener engagement options.

| Reaction | Symbol | Typical visual meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Rocket | 🚀 | Excitement or upward momentum |
| Fire | 🔥 | Attention or high interest |
| Poop | 💩 | Negative or humorous response |
| Red flag | 🚩 | Warning or concern |
| Watchlist | ⭐ | Interest in monitoring the token |

The meanings above describe common visual interpretation. They do not establish genuine user opinion, token quality or future performance.

## Choosing a Reaction Campaign

Reaction selection should match the campaign's intended presentation and the project's communication style.

### Rocket reactions

Rocket reactions are commonly associated with excitement and momentum. They may be selected for a prepared promotional campaign around a legitimate project update or announcement.

### Fire reactions

Fire reactions communicate attention or intensity. As with all paid reactions, they should not be described as proof that independent users support the token.

### Watchlist reactions

Watchlist reactions suggest that a token page is being monitored. Paid watchlist engagement does not establish that unique investors independently chose to follow the project.

### Poop and red-flag reactions

These reaction types carry negative meanings. They should not be used to harass competitors, damage unrelated projects or manufacture misleading negative sentiment.

Projects should confirm the selected icon carefully before ordering. Positive and negative reactions cannot be treated as interchangeable.

## Verifying the Correct DexScreener Page

A reaction campaign must be associated with the intended token page.

Before submitting an order, verify:

- Blockchain network
- Token contract address
- DEX and trading pair
- Pair page URL
- Token name and ticker
- Existing page reactions
- Project branding and links

Projects can have several pools, migrated pairs or deployments on different chains. A matching name or ticker is not enough to identify the correct destination.

## Recording a Reaction Baseline

A baseline makes delivery easier to verify.

Before the campaign starts, record:

- Date and time
- Exact DexScreener page
- Current number for each reaction type
- Requested reaction type
- Requested quantity
- Order reference

Screenshots can support internal records, but contract and pair information should also be saved as text.

## Verifying Delivery

Reaction delivery can be reviewed by comparing the final page with the original baseline.

Record:

- Starting reaction total
- Final reaction total
- Difference between totals
- Campaign start time
- Campaign completion time
- Correct reaction type
- Correct token page

Other visitors may add or remove engagement while a campaign is active. For that reason, records should be treated as practical campaign evidence rather than a complete audit of every individual interaction.

## Reactions Versus DexScreener Trending

DexScreener reactions and DexScreener trending are different promotional products.

| Feature | Reactions | Trending |
|---|---|---|
| Primary effect | Adds selected engagement signals | Increases discovery placement visibility |
| Display area | Token page reactions | Trending or discovery surfaces |
| Published options | Rocket, fire, poop, red flag, watchlist | Placement range determined by the campaign |
| Automatic inclusion of the other service | No | No |

An order for reactions should not be assumed to include a trending position. Likewise, a trending campaign should not be assumed to include reactions unless both are explicitly confirmed.

## What Reactions Can Support

Used carefully, reactions can support:

- A prepared token-page campaign
- Visual engagement around a legitimate announcement
- Comparison of DexScreener page activity before and after promotion
- A broader marketing plan that already includes accurate project information
- Testing how visitors respond after reaching the page

Reaction campaigns work best when the project page is already accurate and ready for additional scrutiny.

## What Reactions Cannot Prove

Paid reactions cannot prove:

- Organic community sentiment
- Unique investor interest
- Real holder growth
- Contract security
- Token legitimacy
- Product-market fit
- Sustainable demand
- Future token value
- Financial performance

They should be measured as a delivered promotional input rather than interpreted as an independent market verdict.

## Avoiding Misleading Reporting

Internal and public reporting should distinguish paid reactions from community-generated engagement.

Accurate reporting may say:

- A paid DexScreener reaction campaign was ordered.
- The requested reaction type was delivered to the recorded page.
- Page engagement changed during the campaign period.

Avoid statements such as:

- Thousands of investors independently reacted.
- Reactions guarantee a trending position.
- Positive icons prove token quality.
- Watchlist reactions equal unique buyers.
- Negative icons prove that another token is unsafe.

Where promotional disclosure is required, clearly identify the activity as paid.

## Negative-Reaction Safety

Poop and red-flag reactions can create a negative impression. They should not be used to coordinate harassment, attack competitors or make unsupported accusations about another project.

Campaign operators should only submit pages they are authorized to promote or manage. DexMoji services should not be used to interfere with unrelated third parties.
